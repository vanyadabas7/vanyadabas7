I'm a B.Com (Honours with Research) graduate, coming from a commerce and research background rather than a computer science one. Along the way, I've spent a lot of time designing surveys, analysing consumer behaviour, and writing research papers — and I realised I enjoy the "figuring out what the numbers mean" part more than anything else.

That curiosity is what's pulling me toward analytics. I'm now building the technical side — Python, SQL, Power BI — to go with the research and business thinking I already have, with the goal of working as a **Business Analyst**.

## 💼 Projects / Case Studies

### 📊 Chatbot Design & Customer Satisfaction (Dissertation)
- **Business problem:** Does the type of chatbot (menu-based, pseudo-conversational, or fully conversational AI) affect how satisfied customers feel when shopping online in India?
- **Approach:** Designed and ran a quantitative survey of 230 respondents, analysed satisfaction patterns across chatbot types.
- **Tools used:** Survey design, statistical analysis, research methodology.

### 🌍 Trade Pattern Analysis
- **Business problem:** How did trade patterns shift across countries and sectors over a multi-year period?
- **Approach:** Conducted an in-depth analysis of trade data for Australia, Belgium, Bulgaria, India, and the USA (2006–08) across food products, printing, and motor vehicle sectors.
- **Tools used:** Data analysis, comparative research.

### 🧴 L'Oréal — Global Brand Strategy Case Study
- **Business problem:** How has L'Oréal built and sustained a global brand across diverse markets?
- **Approach:** Studied and broke down L'Oréal's global branding and market strategies.
- **Tools used:** Case study analysis, strategic frameworks.

### ⭐ Impact of Customer Reviews on Sales
- **Business problem:** Do customer reviews measurably influence sales outcomes?
- **Approach:** Wrote a research paper examining the relationship between customer review sentiment/volume and sales performance.
- **Tools used:** Research & analysis.
